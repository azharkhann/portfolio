Transformation 05 · Industrial Manufacturing

14:02 · a part has no photo and no listing copy. The pipeline drafts both.

A parts spreadsheet becomes a sellable catalog page.

Situation
The client's dealer catalog needed photography and descriptions for parts that had neither. Sourcing and shooting them at scale was slow and expensive.
Mechanism
Two governed pipelines, not one model. GPT-4 drafts descriptions from cleaned part data. Stable Diffusion and ControlNet generate images conditioned on real geometry: edge detection, depth maps, and a 3D color snapshot — never freehand.
Governance
Every generated image and description passes a review gate before publish, bounded to three reprocess passes. Past that, a person builds the listing by hand.
Result
The 2023 POC proved the pipeline; the client rolled it into an MVP for its new B2B dealer ecommerce site.
