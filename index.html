The decisions behind it
01 · Map first, automate second
Built a process register before automating a line: what to orchestrate, what to redesign, what stays human. Cost: a slower start. Return: 35 systems under one governed layer, no rewrite since 2021.
02 · One routing layer
Every system connects through a single hub: 450 workflows composing 3,650 services from one shared library. Cost: every point-to-point shortcut refused. Return: new markets and nine acquisitions land as configuration.
03 · Say no to the client's own purchase
Scored 16 capabilities of their separately-bought Salesforce AI estate, ruled all three nominated use cases unsafe, delivered a phased path, Phase 1 at no new spend. Cost: telling a client no. Return: nothing shipped that would have failed in production.
04 · Design recovery, don't hope
Fail-closed batches, bounded retries, 3,175 typed error codes. Cost: engineering the exceptions before the features. Return: nine in ten thousand reach a person; zero outages in five years.
Govern first. Autonomy is the result.

The decisions behind it
01 · Twin before automation
The prior attempt automated without a documented process and broke on real invoices. Built the twin first, rebuilt on it. Cost: standing still while the twin was built. Return: 65% → 99%, every metric attributable since.
02 · Hold what won't reconcile
A record that will not reconcile is held, never published. Cost: throughput sacrificed to truth. Return: one reconciled base four products read from.
03 · Payment as the byproduct
Benchmarking, forecasts, accruals, and carbon cut from the reconciled base. Cost: building data products a bill-pay operation "didn't need." Return: a moat no competitor can buy.
04 · Onboard by configuration
15,000+ vendors and 300+ layouts as configuration, not code. Cost: a harder platform design up front. Return: a new client in 30 days, not 90.

The decisions behind it
01 · A person on every regulated call
The engine could decide; it doesn't. 100% of regulated decisions keep a human. Cost: a ceiling on the automation rate. Return: full case-level auditability and a system regulators can trust.
02 · Three shared checks, not seven builds
Seven programs reduced to the three activities that recur, built once, inherited by every line of business. Cost: saying no to seven bespoke builds. Return: new regions deploy directly; ~$12M across the portfolio.
03 · Re-sequence before automating
Atomic activities; disputes re-sequenced so denial parameters evaluate first; 12–15 screens collapsed to one workspace. Cost: redesign before any AI. Return: a 21-step review becomes one decision-ready case; handle time down 30–35%.
04 · Governance before expansion
New lines and geographies onboard as configuration; EMEA in design under EU AI Act requirements. Cost: governance work ahead of growth. Return: India replicated to LATAM without rebuilding.
